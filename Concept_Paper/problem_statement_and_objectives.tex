\section{Problem statement}

Waste in Kampala is mostly not separated at source, a large share of it is not collected at all, and what is collected goes to a small number of disposal sites. Hotels and restaurants produce food waste and packaging every day, and unless the waste is separated where it is produced, material that could be recovered is mixed again and joins the rest. Local investigations confirm that restaurant waste in Kampala is often left unsegregated in substandard bins \cite{walina}, even though clean food waste has high potential for alternative circular recovery \cite{ssepuuya}. The collectors who serve these sites work on schedules that have been reported as irregular \cite{mwesigwa}, and they have no way of knowing how full a bin is before they arrive. We expect that bins at some sites are overflowing when the collector comes, while others are emptied when they are half full, and we will check this at the selected sites through observation and interviews. We do not assume that the situation is the same across Uganda.

Earlier prototypes show that automatic identification and remote monitoring can be done. The sources we have seen, however, do not show a prototype that combines four-class sorting, a separate fill level for each compartment, a prediction of the time until a collection threshold is reached, and a record of where the separated waste goes, tested in hotels and restaurants in Uganda. Several of them report no measured results or only results from open datasets. The specific problem of this study is therefore the lack of timely, per-compartment information on what waste has been separated, how full each compartment is and when it will need to be collected. We will develop and test a workflow that supplies this information to hotel staff and collectors under the conditions at selected sites.

\section{Main objective}

To develop and evaluate a smart bin system for waste classification, fill-up prediction and smart disposal in selected hotels and restaurants in Uganda.

\subsection{Specific objectives}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.4em]
\item To investigate existing waste-handling practices and requirements relevant to smart-bin design in selected hotels and restaurants in Kampala, Uganda.
\item To design an AI-enabled IoT smart-bin system integrating local waste classification, automated sorting, compartment fill-level sensing, collection-time estimation and mobile monitoring.
\item To develop a working prototype that classifies supported waste categories, automatically directs waste into designated compartments, monitors fill levels, estimates collection times and provides relevant alerts through a mobile interface.
\item To evaluate the prototype's waste-classification performance, sorting success, fill-level measurement accuracy, collection-estimation performance and alert delivery under controlled conditions.
\end{enumerate}
